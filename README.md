# Greater India

An Indian interdisciplinary research framework unifying cyclotomic polynomial theory, mathematical economics, financial systems, econometrics, institutional design, artificial intelligence, and geopolitical cooperation.

Built upon the flat cyclotomic polynomial **Φ₁₀₂₅(x)**, of degree **800**, this treatise explores a mathematical foundation for modeling Greater India as a 1025-district economic architecture, connecting rigorous algebraic structures with proposed applications in resource allocation, financial stability, public finance, welfare economics, computational science, and peaceful international cooperation.

The framework distinguishes proven mathematical results from hypotheses requiring empirical validation, establishing a research agenda at the intersection of mathematics, economics, governance, and the future of Greater India.
